No. 01 · The Story

A familiar address, after dark.

Tarrant Street has known this room for thirty years.

First it was Butlers, the restaurant, bar and terrace that Arundel grew up with, pouring since 1995. When its doors closed, the town felt it. So when new hands took the keys, they kept the name out of respect and added two words of their own.

Butlers After Dark opened in June 2026 as something a little different: a small, candlelit bar for classic cocktails, good wine and unhurried evenings. Around thirty seats, a quiet soundtrack, and a small enclosed terrace out back.

There is no stage and no fuss. It is simply a grown-up room where the drinks are taken seriously and nothing else is. Whether you come for a negroni after work or a bottle with friends on a Saturday, there is a seat waiting.

No. 04 · Good to Know

House notes, worth knowing.

  1. i.

    Walk-ins welcome

    No bookings, no lists. Just find a seat and settle in. If it’s full, it’s worth the wait.

  2. ii.

    Blue light, always

    Nurses, police, teachers, social workers, fire and armed forces get 10% off, permanently. A small thank-you for looking after the rest of us.

  3. iii.

    Bring the dog

    Arundel is a walking town and we’re a walking-distance bar. Dogs are not just tolerated, they’re expected.

  4. iv.

    Occasions, occasionally

    Not a live-music venue, but the odd evening worth marking does happen. Watch Facebook for word of them.
